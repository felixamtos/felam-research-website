1. Nature of the service

Felam Research Consultancy & Writing Solutions provides research support, consultation, editing, data analysis, methodology guidance, research-tool development, scientific writing assistance and related services. Deliverables are agreed with the client before work begins.

2. Academic integrity

Felam supports learning and legitimate research assistance. Clients remain responsible for understanding their institution's rules, conducting their own academic work where required, approving interpretations, acknowledging assistance appropriately and making final submissions themselves. Services must not be used to misrepresent another person's work as the client's own.

Felam does not knowingly support plagiarism, fabrication of data, falsification of results, impersonation, manipulation of findings to obtain a desired conclusion, or other academic misconduct.

3. Data analysis and interpretation

Statistical or qualitative analysis is based on the data, methods and instructions supplied or agreed. Analytical outputs are not a guarantee of a particular finding, publication, grade, funding decision or research outcome. Clients are responsible for confirming that methods and interpretations meet their institution, journal, funder or regulatory requirements.

4. Client responsibilities

  • Provide accurate project information and lawful access to any data supplied.
  • Obtain required ethical approvals, participant consent and institutional permissions.
  • Remove unnecessary personal identifiers before sharing research datasets whenever practical.
  • Review deliverables and communicate corrections within the agreed review period.
  • Keep copies of original files and maintain appropriate backups.
