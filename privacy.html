Privacy Notice

Effective date: 18 September 2026

Felam Research Consultancy & Writing Solutions respects the privacy of people who contact us or use this website. This notice explains the information we may collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we may receive

  • Contact details such as your name, phone number and email address when you submit an enquiry.
  • Project information that you voluntarily provide, such as research objectives, datasets, deadlines or requested services.
  • Payment confirmation details you send to us when confirming an M-Pesa payment. We do not request or store your M-Pesa PIN.
  • Basic website usage information where analytics are enabled.

2. How we use information

Information is used to respond to enquiries, scope and deliver agreed consultancy services, communicate about projects, verify payments, maintain business records, improve the website and understand website usage where analytics have been enabled.

3. WhatsApp and email

Enquiry forms on this website open WhatsApp with the details you entered. WhatsApp, your mobile network and email provider process information under their own policies. Avoid sending passwords, M-Pesa PINs or other unnecessary sensitive information.

4. Analytics

This website is prepared to support Google Analytics 4 and Cloudflare Web Analytics. Analytics are only active after the relevant account IDs/tokens are configured in analytics.js or enabled through the hosting platform. Cloudflare describes its Web Analytics as privacy-focused and says it does not collect or use visitors' personal data for its analytics product. Google Analytics may process usage information according to Google's terms and privacy documentation.

5. Research materials and confidentiality

Project materials are used only for the agreed consultancy purpose. Clients should provide only information necessary for the work. Where research data contains identifiable participant information, the client remains responsible for ensuring appropriate ethical approval, consent, lawful processing and secure handling arrangements before sharing it.

6. Retention and security

We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, contractual obligations, dispute resolution or legitimate business record-keeping. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices

You may contact Felam to ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask about deletion where applicable. Some records may need to be retained where there is a legitimate legal or contractual reason.
